# Identisure

Identisure provides electronic identification for phone calls, customer service and digital flows, with support for Nordic eIDs: BankID (Sweden and Norway), Freja eID, MitID and FTN. More than 500 companies in the Nordics use the service, including in healthcare, banking, finance, insurance and retail.

Identisure is the Nordic brand of [IDkollen](https://github.com/IDkollen).

## Services

- **TeleID** - Identify customers over phone or chat, no integration needed
- **TeleSign** - Sign agreements and powers of attorney during the call
- **SafeMail** - Secure messages that require eID to read
- **FormID** - Web forms signed with eID
- **REST API** - Authentication, signing, age verification and ID verification in your own systems
